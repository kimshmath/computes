Decider · JavaScript

Induced subgraph decider for C(S0,5)

Decides whether a given graph embeds as an induced subgraph of the curve graph of the five-punctured sphere. Since the complexity is ξ(S0,5) = 2, the Aougab–Biringer–Gaster theorem forces any such graph to be simple and strictly triangle-free. Offers a practical certificate search and an exact certified mode, with built-in examples.
